# Adoption

The Human Index is meant to travel beyond Still Cloud. Adoption should be specific enough that users, employees, researchers, and other organizations can tell what was actually promised.

## Minimum adoption statement

An adopting organization should publish the exact Human Index version; the products, models, APIs, or business units covered; any declared exceptions with reasons; a public contact or issue process for alleged violations; and a link to the organization's completed or adapted Human Index review process.

Suggested language:

> We have adopted Human Index 1.0.0-draft.1 for **[defined product or system]**. This is a self-declaration, not certification by Still Cloud.

For stable releases, replace the draft version with the exact stable version.

## No certification by implication

There is currently no certification program. Do not use language such as "Human Index Certified," "HI Certified," or "Still Cloud Approved" unless a future independent process explicitly creates that status. Listing an organization in [ADOPTERS.md](ADOPTERS.md) records its declaration; it does not verify the organization or endorse all of its practices.

## Forking and adaptation

Organizations may fork or adapt the framework under CC BY 4.0. If the rules are materially changed, the adapted framework should not be represented as an unmodified Human Index release. Preserve attribution and make differences visible.
