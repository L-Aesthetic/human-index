# Sources and Precedents

**Status: bibliography in progress for v1.0.0.**

The stable research notes will cover precedent and evidence from AI risk management, human-computer interaction, autonomy and persuasive technology, algorithmic scoring and profiling, automation bias, recommender systems, child data, inferred sensitive information, AI memory, and digital afterlife research.

This draft intentionally does not treat the existence of a policy framework as evidence that every Human Index rule is empirically proven. Sources will be annotated by what they actually support.
